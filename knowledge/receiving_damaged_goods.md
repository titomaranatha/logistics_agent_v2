# SOP-REC-02 Receiving Damaged Goods

## Inspection at the dock
Inspect every pallet before signing the delivery note. Photograph visible damage from at least two angles. Never sign "clean receipt" for a pallet with crushed corners, torn wrap, wet cartons or leaking product.

## Quarantine and reporting
Move damaged pallets to the quarantine bay (location Q-01) and tag them with a red hold label. Raise a damage report within 4 hours of receipt. Damage over 5% of a delivery, or any damage to temperature-controlled goods, must be escalated to the shift supervisor immediately.

## Carrier claims
Claims must be filed with the carrier within 24 hours of delivery with photos, the signed delivery note and the damage report number. The supervisor owns the claim; operators attach evidence only.
