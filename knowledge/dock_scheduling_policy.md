# SOP-DOCK-01 Dock Scheduling Policy

## Slot rules
Dock slots are 60 minutes. Inbound and outbound carriers must hold a booked slot. A carrier arriving more than 30 minutes late loses the slot and is rebooked at the next free slot.

## Priorities
Priority order for conflicts: temperature-controlled inbound, same-day outbound customer orders, standard inbound, returns. Only a supervisor may override priority.

## Changes and cancellations
Cancellations must be made at least 2 hours before the slot. Late cancellations are logged against the carrier for the monthly performance review.
